Issue badge Identifies the story as covering 0–10 Ten Spiritual Vibrations. Inline badge following the introductory header content. Relative/absolute positioning anchors the badge to the upper-right of the feature header at wider viewport widths. The feature header is the containing block because it uses position: relative. The badge does not create a new stacking context. Below 52rem, absolute positioning disengages and the badge returns to normal flow. Verify at 320px and 1280px that the badge does not cover the title, introduction, or other content.
Story guide Keeps the two story-section navigation links available while reading the feature on wider screens. The story guide appears in normal flow before the article on narrow screens. Sticky positioning keeps the guide near the top of the viewport while the reader scrolls through the story. The story guide is a Grid item inside main. Its z-index uses the documented --layer-sticky token. Below 52rem, sticky positioning disengages and the guide returns to normal flow in the single-column layout. Verify sticky behavior and both navigation links at 1280px. Verify that the guide scrolls normally and does not obscure content at 320px.
Pull quote Emphasizes the statement “Love is what we have when all the questions are over” and creates an editorial focal point. The blockquote remains in normal document flow between the surrounding story paragraphs. At wider viewport widths, a negative inline-start margin creates a controlled overlap beyond the normal article text edge. Relative positioning and the raised layer token control its painting order. The pull quote remains positioned relative to its normal-flow location and uses --layer-raised for its z-index. Below 52rem, the negative margin and positioned enhancement disengage, returning the quote to its normal-flow presentation. At 1280px, verify that the overlap is visible without covering navigation, prose, or creating horizontal scrolling. At 320px, verify that the quote remains within the viewport and does not overlap surrounding content.
Infinity medallion Visually represents 8 — Infinity and reinforces the story's discussion of the infinite Creator. The image remains visible and readable as a normal responsive image without the shape-wrap enhancement. A 50% border radius creates the circular medallion, while shape-outside creates an editorial circular text wrap with additional spacing provided by shape-margin. The image remains in the normal document flow. At wider viewport widths it floats inline-start so surrounding story text can wrap around its circular shape. It does not use a z-index or create an intentional stacking layer. If shape-outside is unsupported or disabled, the text uses the image's normal rectangular float boundary and the story remains complete and readable. Verify the circular border radius and text wrap at 1280px. Disable shape-outside and confirm that all story content remains complete and readable.
Revelation portal Provides a decorative visual connection to 4 — Revelation, which the story describes as a doorway. A simple gold rectangular decorative shape appears before the Revelation paragraph. A clip-path polygon changes the rectangle into a portal-like shape, reinforcing the doorway concept without clipping or hiding essential story content. The decorative element remains in normal document flow and does not use a z-index or create an intentional stacking layer. If clip-path is unsupported or disabled, the decoration returns to its rectangular baseline. The Revelation text remains complete and readable. Verify the portal shape at 1280px. Disable clip-path in Developer Tools and confirm that the decoration becomes rectangular while all story content remains complete and readable.
